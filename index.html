About

Precision. Presence. Performance.

My name is Edmond Onsare, known on stage as Black Monster Model. I welcome you to explore my journey in modeling, judging, mentoring, and training.

My career spans diverse experiences in the world of fashion and creative expression, and I am dedicated to bridging artistic vision with professionalism. I am a passionate and dedicated fashion model, trainer, judge, event organizer, and team builder with extensive experience in various modeling fields.

Modeling is more than just wearing clothes - it is about embodying a vision, bringing creativity to life, and telling stories through movement and expression. I have always been passionate about the art of fashion and performance, and over the years, I have honed my ability to adapt to diverse modeling demands.

Born and raised in Kisii, Kenya, his journey has taken him from local runways to international stages, refining his identity as a model, judge, and mentor.
